About The Platform

Purpose-Built for Idaho Faculty and Student Success

The Student Job Hub was created to give Idaho faculty a direct way to post helpful opportunities that align with student career paths and learning milestones—from early coursework and internships to first career placements across every program we support.

Instructor Managed Student Friendly Career Focused
Universal design details

Large touch targets, readable typography, and consistent interaction patterns lower friction for all users.

How It Works

A simple flow built for busy departments and fast student engagement.

1

Professors Curate Opportunities

Faculty members share high-quality openings from trusted Idaho employers, including clinics, firms, agencies, and campus partners.

2

Post via URL or Template

Users upload a posting URL or create a complete listing using the structured template on the Jobs page.

3

Students Discover and Apply

Idaho students browse by job type and act with confidence because job information is clear and consistently formatted.
